# Mod Information
Noodle Extensions - A mod that was featured alongside EXSI in 2020, its a mod that basically lets you do whatever to the notes or walls in the level, letting them follow a [[X,Y,Z]] position, even using [Easings](https://easings.net) to properly move things in any way you want, even use Time (you can use it in such formats like [[X,Y,Z,T]]) which lets you change how fast objects move, from any time from 0 to 0.5 to 1, (e.g, [[-5,2,0,0],[0,0,0,0.4]])

Chroma - Another mod that lets you basically do anything with said environment, within the boundries of what you have in game, i don't know much about chroma, but i know you can make custom lighting effects, Environments, and custom animations, with just the base game assets, basically you can't just add your own stuff but you can do stuff with the base game assets!

Vivify - As Explained in EXSII, Its a mod that revolutionizes the mapping scene by letting mappers drop in a unity assetbundle and load that into beatsaber, basically letting mappers go extra crazy with their maps, it can even go alongside chroma and Noodle!
